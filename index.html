Meet the Ferrari SF-26.

The SF-26 is Ferrari's 2026 Formula 1 car, built for a new era of technical regulations. This simple page highlights a few ideas that make an F1 car interesting to study.

What makes an F1 car special?

An F1 car is a collection of connected engineering choices. The shape, power unit, tires, and aerodynamics all have to work together.

01

Aerodynamics

Wings and bodywork help manage airflow and create the downforce needed for a car to corner quickly.

02

Power unit

The SF-26 belongs to a new 2026 rules era, where changes to the power unit are an important part of the design challenge.

03

Driver + team

A fast car still needs a driver, engineers, mechanics, and a race strategy working together throughout a Grand Prix weekend.

Look closer

Three things to notice

Low and wide

F1 cars are shaped close to the ground so engineers can carefully control airflow around the car.

Lots of data

Teams use sensors and timing information to understand how the car is behaving and where it can be improved.

Designed for rules

Every generation of F1 car has to fit a detailed technical rule set, so regulation changes can reshape the entire design.
